## 👋 About Me

I'm Krish — a Computer Science undergrad specialising in AI & ML at Ramdeobaba University, Nagpur, with a 9.02 CGPA and a habit of building things that actually get used. My current obsession is **HisaabKitaab**, a full-stack PWA I built from scratch to solve a genuinely frustrating problem: splitting group expenses fairly without endless back-and-forth. It handles real-time balances, OCR receipt scanning, UPI-linked settlements, and Razorpay payments — built entirely for Indian users, by an Indian developer. Outside of shipping code, I grind through Competitive Programming across LeetCode, Codeforces, and CodeChef with a particular focus on Dynamic Programming, Monotonic Stacks, and Graph patterns. I move fast, learn faster, and I'm genuinely convinced that with the right tools and enough obsession, you can build production-grade software in your second year of college. When I'm away from a screen, I'm on a football pitch, editing bike trip videos with friends, putting in hours at the gym, or just finding someone new to talk to — collecting perspectives is my favourite hobby.
