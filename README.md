## Hi, I'm Krish

I'm a B.Tech student in Computer Science (AI & ML) at Ramdeobaba University, Nagpur. I like taking a problem from "this is annoying" to a working product that real people can open on their phone: the idea, the design, the code, the tests and the words on the screen.

I keep coming back to everyday and public problems in India: splitting a bill without arguments, a route that avoids a flooded road, a map that shows whether a road really got built, a ranked list that tells an officer which public works to inspect first.

Three habits show up in everything I make:

- **Plain language.** If a person who is not technical cannot follow it, it is not finished.
- **Honest limits.** A good tool says what it cannot do. My satellite project answers "Nothing you can claim here." when its photos cannot support a claim.
- **Made for everyone.** Keyboard, screen reader, slow phone, dark and light themes.

I move fast, I ship, and I write things down. I build with AI coding tools, often by speaking my prompts instead of typing them.

## What I'm building now

### GrahSaboot: satellite proof for any place

<a href="https://grahsaboot.grahsaboot.workers.dev">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Krishpotanwar/grahsaboot/main/docs/screenshots/globe.png" alt="The GrahSaboot globe with live satellites" width="640" />
</a>

A free website where you pick a place and get real, dated satellite photos (Sentinel-2) with the clouds checked. Put a "before" photo next to an "after" photo, pin the dates that matter, and save a report. Every photo gets a digital fingerprint so anyone can check it later, and the site is upfront about what 10-metre photos cannot show. Built with TypeScript, React, MapLibre and a Cloudflare Worker, and tested in real browsers and for accessibility.

[Live site](https://grahsaboot.grahsaboot.workers.dev) · [Code](https://github.com/Krishpotanwar/grahsaboot)

### HisaabKitaab: splitting group expenses, built for India

A full-stack Progressive Web App for friends, roommates and trips. It shows real-time balances and who owes whom, scans receipts with OCR, and supports UPI-linked settlements and Razorpay payments. No install needed: it works in any phone browser, with dark mode.

[Live site](https://hisab-rust.vercel.app) · [Code](https://github.com/Krishpotanwar/Hisab)
